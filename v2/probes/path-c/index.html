MODULE 4Product table — the geometric product

Rows and columns are trigrams (their blades). Click a row trigram and a column trigram: the cell shows their geometric product (blade + sign). Green cells: sign +1. Red cells: sign −1. Order matters: e1·e2 = e12 but e2·e1 = −e12.
